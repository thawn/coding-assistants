## 1\. Principal Investigator / Scientific Reviewer

Focus:

* scientific merit
* clarity of hypothesis
* methodology
* feasibility
* measurable outcomes

## 2\. Clinical Practitioner (Oncologist / Nurse Lead)

Focus:

* clinical usefulness
* workflow fit
* patient safety
* risk of missed escalation
* interpretability

## 3\. Data Protection / Compliance Officer

Focus:

* GDPR / data minimization
* sensitive health data handling
* storage / access / logging
* external APIs / model hosting
* re-identification risk

## 4\. Research Ethics / IRB Reviewer

Focus:

* informed consent
* vulnerable populations
* fairness / bias
* human oversight
* harm mitigation

## 5\. Patient Advocate / Patient Representative

Focus:

* accessibility
* trust / transparency
* burden on patients
* inclusion of special situations
* emotional and psychosocial impact
